CSV format, scoring and privacy details Required columns: lead_id, company, deal_value, currency, stage, last_contact_date, owner, win_probability_percent, next_action_due_date. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD; one currency per file. Maximum 1 MB / 2,000 opportunities. Duplicate IDs, invalid dates, mixed currencies and malformed files are rejected. Open opportunities are ranked by probability-weighted deal value, with transparent heuristic boosts for overdue/stale/missing tasks. Ranking is not a validated closing probability or recovered revenue estimate. CSV data is processed in this browser tab, not uploaded, stored, or sent to any AI provider.
